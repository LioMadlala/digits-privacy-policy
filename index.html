Digits — Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Digits ("the app") is a phone-contact and encounter manager. This page explains what information the app accesses, how it's stored, and who it's shared with.

Summary

  • Digits stores your data locally on your device, in a private database that is not uploaded to any server operated by us.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not run analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.
  • Digits does not have user accounts, and we do not operate any backend server that receives your contacts, location, or notes.

Information the app accesses

Contacts. With your permission, Digits reads your phone's contacts so you can view, tag, and organize them inside the app, and — only when you explicitly choose to — writes changes back to a contact on your device (for example, cleaning up a messy name or reformatting a phone number). Before Digits changes or deletes anything on your phone, it saves a private backup copy on your device so you can review or restore it. This backup never leaves your device.

Location. With your permission, Digits uses your device's location to let you record where you met someone (an "encounter") and to show that place on a map. Location is only requested when you actively use these features — Digits does not track your location in the background.

Map tiles and address lookups. To display maps and turn map coordinates into readable addresses (or an address you type into a search box into map coordinates), the app sends the relevant coordinates or search text to third-party mapping services (map tiles are served by CARTO, built on OpenStreetMap data; address lookups use your device's built-in geocoding service). These requests are limited to the coordinates/text needed to complete the lookup and are not linked to your identity by us.

WhatsApp. If you tap "Message on WhatsApp" on a contact, Digits opens WhatsApp with that one phone number via a standard deep link. Digits does not scan, check, or report on which of your contacts have WhatsApp.

Where your data is stored

All app data you create in Digits — contacts you've organized, tags, notes, and encounter locations — is stored in a local database on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this information. We do not have a copy of it.

Data we don't collect

  • No account registration, so there's no email/password on file with us.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are included in the app.
  • No sale or sharing of your contacts, location, or notes to third parties for marketing purposes.

Your choices

  • You can deny or later revoke the Contacts and Location permissions at any time from your device's system settings; the relevant features will simply be unavailable until access is granted again.
  • You can turn off the in-app "contacts tidy-up" suggestion entirely from within that feature ("Never ask me again").
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored Digits data from your device.

Children's privacy

Digits is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
